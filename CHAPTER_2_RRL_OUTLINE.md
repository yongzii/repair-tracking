## 2. Best Writing Pattern per Paragraph

Each paragraph should follow this flow:

1. Introduce the theme.
2. Cite 2 to 4 related studies.
3. Compare their findings.
4. End with a limitation, gap, or transition.

Simple formula:

`Theme -> Evidence from studies -> Comparison -> Gap`

Example pattern:

"Recent studies on web-based management systems show that digital platforms improve record organization, monitoring, and reporting efficiency. Several studies also found that replacing manual processes with centralized web-based systems reduces delays and improves data accessibility. However, many of these studies focus on general office, academic, or retail contexts rather than electronics repair operations. This indicates a gap in systems that specifically integrate repair job tracking, customer updates, payment monitoring, and spare parts management in one platform."

## 4. Suggested RRL Flow for Your Topic

This is the logic you can follow in writing:

1. Web-based systems help centralize records and improve operational efficiency.
2. Tracking systems improve monitoring of transactions and workflow progress.
3. Inventory systems improve control over stock and parts usage.
4. Payment and financial monitoring systems improve accountability and reporting.
5. Customer tracking and transparency improve service quality and trust.
6. Existing studies rarely combine all these functions into one system specifically for electronics repair shops in a local setting like Surigao City.

## 5. Research Gap Direction

You can build your Chapter 2 ending around this idea:

"Although previous studies discuss web-based management systems, inventory systems, payment monitoring, and tracking platforms, many of them focus on academic institutions, government offices, retail operations, or logistics services. There is still limited recent literature on an integrated web-based repair service management system specifically designed for electronics repair shops that combines job order management, repair status tracking, customer-facing updates, payment monitoring, and spare parts management in one platform. This gap supports the need for the present study in the context of electronics repair shops in Surigao City."
